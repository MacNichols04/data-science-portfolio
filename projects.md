## Project 1: College Football Recruiting and Team Success

**Research Question:** What is the relationship between a college football team's recruiting strength and its on-field success among FBS teams?

This project examines whether stronger recruiting classes are associated with higher winning percentages and stronger overall team ratings among FBS college football programs from 2021 through 2025.
